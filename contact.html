Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How do I register for a course?

You can register for courses by filling out our contact form, calling our admissions office, or visiting our facility in person. Once we receive your inquiry, our team will guide you through the registration process and help you select the right course based on your needs and skill level.

Do you offer trial lessons?

Yes! We offer a 30-minute free trial lesson for new students. This allows you to meet the instructor, try the instrument, and get a feel for our teaching approach before committing to a full course. You can book your free trial lesson through our website or by contacting our admissions office.

What are your payment options?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and checks. Payment plans are available for longer courses, allowing you to pay in monthly installments. We also offer family discounts and referral bonuses. Full details about payment options will be provided during registration.

Do I need to own an instrument to take lessons?

While having your own instrument is beneficial for practice, it's not required to start. We provide instruments for use during lessons at our facility. For those who wish to purchase an instrument, our instructors can provide recommendations based on your skill level and budget. We also have a rental program for select instruments.

What is your make-up lesson policy?

We understand that schedules can change. If you need to miss a lesson, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance, and we'll be happy to reschedule your lesson at a convenient time within the same month. Each student is allowed two make-up lessons per term. Lessons canceled with less than 24 hours' notice may not be eligible for rescheduling.

Do you have a student showcase or recitals?

Yes! We organize quarterly student recitals where our students can showcase their progress and perform in front of an audience. These recitals are optional but highly encouraged as they provide valuable performance experience. We also host an annual showcase event featuring our advanced students and faculty performances.
