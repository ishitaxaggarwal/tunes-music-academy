Our Story

Inspiring musical excellence since 2008

Our Mission

"To inspire and nurture musical talent through exceptional education, fostering creativity, confidence, and lifelong appreciation for music."

At Tunes Music Academy, we believe that music education goes beyond learning notes and techniques. It's about discovering a passion, building confidence, and developing discipline that extends into all areas of life.

Our comprehensive approach combines technical skill development with creative expression, music theory, and performance opportunities. We are committed to providing a supportive environment where students of all ages and abilities can thrive.

Our Journey

The story behind Tunes Music Academy

2008

The Beginning

Founded by concert pianist Sapan Sharma with just two teaching rooms and a vision to create an inclusive music education center.

2011

Expanding Our Reach

Expanded to a larger facility with 8 teaching studios, a small recital hall, and introduced group classes for young beginners.

2015

Growth & Recognition

Recognized as a leading music school in the region. Added recording studio facilities and launched our annual music festival.

2018

A Decade of Excellence

Celebrated our 10th anniversary with the opening of our current state-of-the-art facility , and digital lab.

2022

Innovation in Education

Launched our comprehensive online learning platform to complement in-person lessons and reach students beyond our local community.

Today

Continuing Our Mission

We continue to grow while maintaining our commitment to personalized, high-quality music education.
